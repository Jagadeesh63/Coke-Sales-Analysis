# Coke-Sales-Analysis

Project Overview:

The Dataset contains 1000+ rows of monthly invoices. This is the core dataset with granular 2021 Coca-Cola beverage sales records across retailers (Sodapop, BevCo, FizzySip, DreamCo), regions (Northeast, South, West, Midwest), and cities (New York, Houston, San Francisco, etc.). Key columns include Retailer, Retailer ID, 
Invoice Date, Region/State/City, Beverage Brand (Coca-Cola, Diet Coke, Sprite, etc.), Price per Unit, Units Sold, Total Sales (Price × Units), 
Operating Profit, Operating Margin, and Month (1-12).

Project Structure:

The Excel workbook contains following sheets.

Raw transactional data including:
1. Retailer details
2. Invoice date, Region, State, City
3. Beverage Brand
4. Price per Unit, Units Sold, Total Sales
5. Operating Profit, Operating Margin
6. Month

1. Easy: 
    Created visuals using Bar Chart, Pie Chart, Pie Chart - 3D. 
    In this sheet, mostly focuses on Month, Region, Retailer, Beverage Brand, Units Sold and Total Sales columns to create the visuals.
           i. Sales per Month
           ii. Sales by Region
           iii. Sales by Retailer
           iv. Sales by Brand
           v. Units Sold per Brand 

2. Moderate:
    Created visuals using Bar Chart, Line Chart. 
    In this sheet, mostly focuses on Month, Region, Beverage Brand, Units Sold and Total Sales columns to create the visuals. We used slicer to filter the data.
           i. Sales by Brand and Month
           ii. Sales by Brand and Region
           iii. Monthly Sales Trend by Beverage Brand

3. Hard:
    Created visuals using Bar Chart, Line Chart. 
    In this sheet, mostly focuses on Month, Region, Retailer, Beverage Brand, Units Sold and Total Sales columns to create the visuals.
           i. Sales Trend for Region by Brand 
           ii. Sum of Units sold by Retailer and Beverage Brand
